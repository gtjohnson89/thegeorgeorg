Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

The George Org (“we”) operates thegeorgeorg.org and related products including mash.baby, Mutiny (mutiny.football), and d-Tech. This policy describes how we handle information, including phone numbers used for customer text messages.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as name, email address, and phone number.
  • Account and product information when you use Mutiny, mash.baby, or d-Tech.
  • Message content and delivery status when we text you (for example, that a message was delivered or that you replied STOP).
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited on this website.

How we use it

  • To operate our products and provide customer support.
  • To send texts you asked for — account notices, support replies, and product updates. See our text messaging terms.
  • To improve the site and keep it secure.

Texts and phone numbers

We only text numbers that opted in. We do not sell phone numbers or SMS opt-in data. We do not share them with third parties for their own marketing. Carriers and our texting provider see numbers and message metadata as needed to deliver texts. You can opt out anytime by replying STOP.

Sharing

We use service providers (hosting, email, SMS) that process data on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law, or if we sell or transfer the business, in which case this policy still applies until you are notified of a change.

Retention

We keep contact and messaging records as long as needed to provide the service, honor opt-outs, and meet legal requirements, then delete or de-identify them.

Your choices

Email us at hello@thegeorgeorg.org to ask what we have, correct it, or delete it, subject to the law. For texts, reply STOP or HELP.

Children

mash.baby is made for toddlers to play with a parent. We do not market to children by text. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website or our SMS program.
