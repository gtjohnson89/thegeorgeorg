Text Messaging Terms

Last updated: September 6, 2026

The George Org sends customer text messages about mash.baby, Mutiny, and d-Tech. By opting in, you agree to these terms and our privacy policy.

Program

The George Org Alerts — informational texts from The George Org, including account notices, customer support replies, and product updates for mash.baby, Mutiny, and d-Tech.

How you opt in

We only text you if you asked us to — for example by entering your number in a product, checking a box that says you want texts, or texting a keyword we publish. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Frequency

Message frequency varies. You may get a few messages when you create an account, when you need support, or when a product you use has an update. We do not send a daily marketing drip.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier’s plan governs those charges. The George Org does not charge a separate fee for the texts themselves.

Opt out and help

  • Reply STOP to opt out. You will get one confirmation text. We will not text that number again unless you opt in later.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email hello@thegeorgeorg.org.
  • Supported carriers vary; delivery is not guaranteed if your carrier blocks the number.

What we do not do

We do not sell your phone number. We do not share opt-in data with third parties for their marketing. We do not send texts to numbers we rented or scraped.
